Offline globe data for FireflyToolbox

Geometry: world-atlas 2.0.2, countries-110m.json (Natural Earth 4.1.0).
https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas
Label coordinates and name fallback: Natural Earth vector v5.1.2, ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson, NAME_ZH, NAME_EN, ISO_A2_EH, LABEL_X, LABEL_Y.
https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Chinese short display names use the build environment's Intl.DisplayNames (zh-CN); common search aliases are maintained locally.

Natural Earth data is public domain:
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
world-atlas is redistributed under its ISC license, included with this bundle.

This is a coarse browsing dataset, not an exhaustive administrative atlas or a surveying/navigation service. Small islands and shorelines may be omitted at 1:110m scale.
